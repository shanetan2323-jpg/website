🍲 Reducing Food Waste to End Hunger

By: Tan Yi Thong 3A2

1. The Reality of Food Waste

In Malaysia, thousands of tonnes of food are wasted every day.

📊 Malaysia Statistics

Daily food waste in Malaysia: 17,000 tonnes

Edible food wasted daily: 4,000 tonnes

Source: SWCorp Malaysia

2. Root Causes of Hunger & Food Waste

  • Over-buying: Buying too much food without planning.
  • Ugly Food: Throwing away fruits or vegetables just because of their shape.
  • Over Cooking: Cooking too much and throwing away the leftovers.
  • Taking Too Much:Taking too much in buffet and throwing away the leftovers

3. Take Action & Local Food Banks
